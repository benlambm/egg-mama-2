    How to Play

    • ▲Swipe up (or ↑ / Space) to jump
    • ◀Swipe left (or ←) to brake and back up
    • ▶Swipe right (or →) to push ahead
    • ◻Pause button (or Esc / P) pauses; M mutes
    • Ordinary eggs are points; a golden egg hatches a duckling (up to five)
    • Ducklings are extra lives; a shield absorbs the next hit first
    • Checkpoint flags save your journey; fall or get hit with no ducklings and you retry the last flag
